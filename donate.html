Why we ask for donations.

Cold Case Checker is free for everyone: families, investigators, reporters, and internet sleuths. We want to keep it that way.

We run on a slim budget

Every case file on this site is researched, sourced, and checked again over time. We look for new arrests, new reports, rewards, and records, and we correct the page when something changes. That work takes time and has real costs, and we do it on a slim budget. Any help we receive makes a difference.

What your support helps fund

Keeping case files current

Checking each case for new developments, adding new cases, and correcting records when the facts change.

Running the website

Hosting, the domain, the tip and contact forms, and the tools we use to research, build, and share case files.

Records and research

Costs that come with digging into a case, such as public-records requests and copies of documents.

Helping solve cases

Supporting efforts to crack cold cases and to find missing persons, so that families can get answers.

Good to know

  • Not tax-deductible. As of now, donations to Cold Case Checker are not tax-deductible.

  • Other ways to help. You can also help by sharing case files, sending a sourced tip through the clue form on any case page, or submitting a case we have not covered.
  • If you have information about a case, please also contact the investigating agency listed on that case's page.
