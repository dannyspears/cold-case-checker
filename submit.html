Cold CaseChecker

Submit a cold case.

Old or new, famous or forgotten. Tell us about an unsolved murder or a missing person in the United States and point us to sources. We review every submission before anything is published.

Before you submit.
  • This is not a tip line. If you know something that could help solve a crime, contact the investigating agency or tips.fbi.gov.
  • Include at least one published source (news article, court record, agency page). We do not publish claims we cannot trace.
  • Do not name a living person as a suspect unless an agency or a reliable news outlet already has. We will not publish accusations.
  • Please do not send graphic images. Photos of victims, missing people or suspects must be free to reuse, or sent by the family with permission.

Facts only, in your own words. 2–6 sentences is plenty.

Links to news articles, court records, or agency pages. One per line.
