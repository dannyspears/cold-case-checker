Every cold case deserves a second look.

Cold Case Checker gathers news, official records, and public databases on the best-known unsolved murders and missing-person cases in the United States. Each file lays out what happened, who the victims or missing people were, every named suspect, and the evidence for and against them. Then it offers a hypothesis and flags the loose ends that may have been missed.
