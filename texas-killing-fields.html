The Texas Killing Fields: a field, four women, and decades of other cases that were never solved.

Along Interstate 45 southeast of Houston, more than 30 girls and young women have been found dead since the early 1970s. A 25-acre field off Calder Road in League City is the best known site, but the name covers a much wider set of cases. A few have been solved. Most have not.

Case file at a glance

The Killing Fields is not one case but dozens, solved and unsolved, linked by a place and a pattern of victims. The latest developments are a rare mix of arrest, conviction, and a prime suspect's death, and they show how much remains open.

  3. A prime suspect is dead.Clyde Hedrick, long considered the prime suspect in the four Calder Road deaths, died by suicide in March 2026 before a grand jury presentation.
  4. One man is convicted, in one death.James Elmore Jr. was convicted of manslaughter in Laura Miller's death on Sept 2, 2026 and sentenced to 20 years.

What happened

Dates come from court and news records. Sources differ on some details, which are noted.

1971

The first disappearances

Colette Wilson, 13, disappears in June near Alvin; her body is found in November. Rhonda Johnson, 14, and Sharon Shaw, 13, disappear in August; their remains are found in 1972 near Clear Lake. Debbie Ackerman and Maria Johnson, both 15, vanish in November after being seen in Galveston.

1974

Michael Self convicted

Michael Lloyd Self is convicted of Shaw's murder. He later says his confession was coerced and dies in custody in 2000. Officials said after his death that they believed he had been wrongly convicted.

1984 – 1991

The Calder Road field

Four women are found in the League City field: Laura Miller, Heide Fye Villareal, Audrey Cook, and Donna Prudhomme. Miller, 16, had been missing for 17 months when her body was found in 1986.

1986

Shelley Sikes

Shelley Sikes, 19, disappears in May; her body has never been found. John King and Gerald Zwarst are convicted of aggravated kidnapping in 1998.

1997 – 2016

More victims found

Laura Smither, 12, is found in 1997. Remains of Kelli Cox, 20, and Jessica Cain, 17, are found in 2016.

2012 – 2014

Convictions in other cases

Kevin Edison Smith is convicted in 2012 of the capital murder of Krystal Baker, 13. Clyde Hedrick is convicted of manslaughter in 2014 in the death of Ellen Beason.

April 2019

Genealogy identifies victims

Audrey Lee Cook and Donna Prudhomme are identified using genetic genealogy. Other unidentified victims, including Michelle Garvey (2014) and the Clouse family members (2021), are also named.

2022

Reece pleads guilty

William Lewis Reece pleads guilty to the murders of Laura Smither, Kelli Cox, and Jessica Cain and receives life terms. Netflix releases a docuseries on the Killing Fields.

March – April 2026

Hedrick dies; Elmore arrested

District attorneys say they plan to seek indictments against Hedrick. He dies by suicide before the grand jury presentation. In April, his longtime associate James Elmore Jr. is arrested and charged in two of the Calder Road cases.

June 12, 2026

A new search

Texas EquuSearch volunteers search the field off Calder Drive after Elmore said another body was buried there. Nothing is found.

Sept 2–3, 2026

Elmore convicted

A Galveston County jury convicts Elmore of manslaughter in Laura Miller's death and sentences him to the maximum 20 years.

Latest development: Hedrick, Elmore, and the 2026 search

The latest news is the rare case where a prime suspect died and an associate was convicted, but only in one death.

Not an accusation. Clyde Hedrick died before any charge in the Calder Road deaths. James Elmore Jr. was convicted of manslaughter in one death, not murder, and denies involvement in the others. Neither has been convicted of the four Calder Road murders.

What happened in 2026

  • March: The district attorney's office said it would seek grand jury indictments against Clyde Hedrick, 72. He died by suicide before the presentation.
  • April: His longtime associate James Elmore Jr. was arrested and charged in the Laura Miller and Audrey Cook cases, including evidence tampering.
  • June 12: Texas EquuSearch searched a field off Calder Drive after Elmore said another body was buried there. Nothing was found.
  • Sept 2–3: A jury convicted Elmore of manslaughter in Miller's death and sentenced him to 20 years. The Galveston County DA, appointed in 2025, has said he will look harder at the cases.

What would settle it

  1. Re-examine Hedrick's link to the other three women using records, physical evidence, and Elmore's statements.
  2. Genealogy on any biological evidence from the Calder Road victims.
  3. Test Stallings's confession against the facts of Prudhomme's death.
Cold Case Checker's assessment

One conviction, many open files

We put the chance that Clyde Hedrick was involved in at least one more Calder Road death at roughly 70%, given the investigators' stated belief and Elmore's statements, but with no trial and no physical proof. We put the chance that all four Calder Road deaths are ever formally resolved at roughly 20%.

Our hypothesis

Editorial reading of public records. A working theory, not a finding, and it names no individual.

Working theory · confidence stated per point

Several offenders over decades, and one small group tied to the Calder Road field

  1. This is a place that attracted more than one killer. The cases span 1971 to 2016 and involve different methods and victims. Investigators say more than one person is responsible.
  2. The Calder Road deaths likely involve a small group. The associates who disposed of bodies, and the one who died before charge, point to a circle, not a lone offender.
  3. Genealogy has worked for victims, not yet for killers. The next step is applying the same tools to suspect DNA.
  4. Hedrick's death is a setback for the truth. No trial means no sworn account, so remaining evidence matters more.
Multiple offenders across the corridor Calder Road four linked to one circle Hedrick involved in more than Miller Another body in the field All Calder Road cases resolved

Solved cases

Golden State Killer

Genealogy matched crime-scene DNA to relatives and led to a 2018 arrest. The lesson: it worked once physical DNA existed. Whether any exists here is unknown.

Gilgo Beach

A suspect was charged in 2023 after investigators revisited old evidence and linked it to new data. The lesson: multi-victim corridors can break when old files are reexamined.

Where

The main site is a field off Calder Road (now Calder Drive) in League City, Texas, in Galveston County. The wider corridor runs along I-45 between Houston and Galveston.

Open in maps: Calder Drive, League City · I-45 corridor. The field is private property with memorials; please do not trespass. We show no photographs of the site.
