The Netherland murders: a teacher and her 16-year-old daughter, and a black Impala on video.

On April 21 or 22, 2014, Kathy Netherland, 48, a widowed special-education teacher, and her daughter Samantha, 16, were found dead at their home on Springfield Road outside Bardstown, Kentucky. No one has been charged.

What happened

Details come from a 2018 InsideHook feature and a 2018 LEX18 report. Sources give April 21 or April 22.

April 21 to 22, 2014

The killings

Kathy Netherland and her daughter Samantha are killed at their home at 5120 Springfield Road, near State Routes 150 and 605 outside Bardstown. Kentucky State troopers find them on the morning of April 22.

April 2014

The injuries

According to InsideHook, Kathy was shot several times, Samantha suffered blows to the head, and both had neck wounds.

April 21, 2014

The Impala

A gas-station camera records a black Chevrolet Impala near the area. Police say the driver may have information.

Later

Reward

Reports in search results say the reward was raised from $2,500 to $50,000. We could not confirm that. -Maybe

Aug 13, 2018

Family statement

The family posts that there is no evidence linking the case to the Ellis killing, the Rogers disappearance, or the Ballard shooting.

Solved cases

Local cases that moved after decades.

Carol Sue Klaber (Boone County, KY, 1976)

Investigators say a private genealogy investigation helped them identify Thomas Dunaway, who died in 1990, as the man who killed the 16-year-old. He cannot be indicted, so prosecutors sent a letter saying he would have been charged. The lesson: genealogy can name a killer after decades, even when no trial is possible.

Forensic genetic genealogy training

In 2024, local investigators were being trained in the technique, which has already helped solve Cincinnati-area cases, according to FOX19. The lesson: the tool is now local, so cases with usable DNA have a real path forward.

Golden State Killer

Old crime-scene DNA matched relatives in a public database and led to a 2018 arrest. The lesson: a usable sample can still work after decades.
